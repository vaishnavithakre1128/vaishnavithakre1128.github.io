Projects

Wearable Health Monitor

Smart wristband that tracks heart rate, HRV, SpO2, estimated blood pressure, stress, steps, calories and distance, with zone localization and peer-to-peer distance over a Wi-Fi mesh.

  • Pulse peak detection with threshold edge detection, smoothed using an exponential moving average.
  • SpO2 from the red/IR absorbance ratio; HRV from the standard deviation of inter-beat intervals; stress mapped from HRV thresholds.
  • Shared I2C bus for pulse oximeter, temperature, motion sensors and OLED.

IoT Sensor Box for Mining & Industry

Raspberry Pi monitoring unit combining environmental, gas, dust and vibration sensing with live camera streaming and Wi-Fi based zone location.

  • BMP280 (temperature, pressure) and ADS1115 ADC for MQ gas and GP2Y1010 dust sensors over I2C.
  • SW-420 vibration input on GPIO; sensor data published over MQTT, video streamed over UDP.
  • Approximate location from Wi-Fi BSSID and RSSI; central server dashboard.
Raspberry PiPythonMQTTUDPI2CADS1115

IoT Solar Power Monitoring & Optimization

B.Tech final-year team project (4 members). An ESP32 monitors panel voltage, load voltage and light intensity, and switches between solar panels and super-capacitor storage through relays to keep load power steady.

  • ADC-based sampling and threshold-triggered actuation.
  • Local LCD display and cloud upload for remote monitoring.
ESP32Super-capacitorsRelaysLCDCloud IoT

Shaft & Bearing Assembly with Articulated Robot

Industrial training mini project (BEST, BIT Wardha). A Mitsubishi articulated robot performs pick-and-place between a loading cylinder and a pneumatic press-fit cylinder for consistent assembly.

  • Programmed with RT ToolBox3 and teach pendant.
  • Also covered palletizing and weight-based sorting.

Multi-Level Automated Car Parking (PLC)

Team project (4 members) in industrial training. A PLC detects vehicles with proximity sensors, identifies vehicle type by weight, controls entry/exit gates and shows free slots on an HMI.

  • Up/down counters for slot tracking, latching logic for gates, interlocks when full.
